# Chicago food and bars

Home base: **South Loop**. Recs as Google Maps walking links that open the **place**, not a street pin.

Taste: pub food (fish and chips, English pies), neighborhood gastropubs and real pubs. Michelin is a plus, not a filter. Dives are fine if food or vibe is actually good.

## Regulars

- **Printers Row Wine Shop** — 719 S Dearborn. Happy hour. Not Vitalogy next door.
- **Third Rail Tavern** — 1133 W Madison. Always before United Center. Not Ryota next door.
- **The Staley** — 1736 S Michigan
- **Half Sour** — South Loop
- **Buttercup** — 75 E 16th (cafe by day, bar at night)
- **Spoke & Bird** — South Loop (repeat receipts summer 2026)

## Loved

- **Chief O’Neill’s** — 3471 N Elston. 30 Aug 2026, both loved it (Irish band, pot pie, Guinness).
- **Armitage Alehouse** — pike fish and chips
- **Longman & Eagle**
- **Pleasant House Pub** — Pilsen, Royal pies

## Other visits

Giant (with Hannah), Italian Homemade Company, The Publican, Monteverde, The Hideout, Duke of Perth, Waveland Bowl, Trivoli Tavern, Lou Malnati’s West Loop.

## Skip

Pippin’s Tavern, Bar Louie Printers Row, Devil Dawgs on State.

## Rated well

Navigator Taproom, Kroll’s South Loop.
